## Principles for the launch
- Say what exists; link to the [known limitations](https://bsrikumar855-dot.github.io/ClaudeCODE-Autonomous-skills/faq.html#limitations).
- No fake stars, reviews, backlinks, benchmarks or endorsements. Don't ask people to upvote or star.
- Post each draft once, where it fits the community's rules, and answer questions honestly.
